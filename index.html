Version 1.0.2 is out!

  • The supervisor and the speaker can now share their screen, an application, or a browser tab with the interpreter, including audio. Unlike the video player whose audio is not recorded, the audio shared via screen share is included in the recording as a separate track.
  • It is possible to select headphones or speakers other than the default ones.
  • The control panel settings are now saved.
  • New audio or video devices are automatically detected.
  • Switch role feature for interpreter and speaker.
